Tunk demo page — four directions

Four ways to open the page, all wearing the brand captured in _brand.md. Copy is real, taken from the site and from the measured report. The screenshots are the shipped app.

source: site/index.html, reports/QA-FINAL.md · surface: demo page hero and tour · 2026-09-11

A

Gesture first

Optimises for the one idea a stranger needs. Gives up proof and product detail above the fold.

macOS menubar utility

Knock twice on your MacBook.

Tunk feels it through the accelerometer already inside the machine and fires the shortcut you chose. Like Back Tap on iPhone, for a laptop.

Download for Apple silicon See the measurements

Works: one sentence carries the whole product, and the art says the physics without a hand in frame. Risks: nothing on screen proves it works, so a sceptic bounces before the evidence. Pick when the audience has never heard of Back Tap.

B

App first

Optimises for "this is real software". Gives up the emotional hook of the gesture.

Three screens, no manual.

Turn it on, watch taps land, choose what a double tap does. Everything else lives under Advanced.

General · live tap monitor

Actions · hotkey or Shortcut

Calibration · learned from your taps

Works: the real UI is the strongest proof available, and all three screens fit one row. Risks: a visitor who does not yet want the product is being shown settings. Pick when the traffic already knows what Tunk is.

The harness reports FAIL on lap detection and INCOMPLETE where nothing was recorded. Both numbers are on this page because hiding the second one would make the first worthless.

Works: publishing the failing number is the most persuasive thing here, and it matches the voice in the identity. Risks: a general visitor reads a table and leaves. Pick when the page is for Hacker News rather than for buyers.

D

Setup first

Optimises for the thing that actually loses people: two permissions. Gives up the hook and the proof.

Running in four steps.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Tunk into Applications.
  2. Open it. It lives in the menu bar, no Dock icon.
  3. Grant two permissions. The window opens the right pane for each.
  4. Double-tap the case. The ring lights up for each knock it hears.

Download the DMG 4.6 MB · Apple silicon · macOS 13+

Works: answers the question that actually defeated the first install, and the download sits with the steps. Risks: leads with admin, which reads as friction before any desire exists. Pick when the page is a download page rather than a pitch.

Decision

  • If the page must convert a stranger, pick A, with B directly below it.
  • If it is shown to people who already know the idea, pick B.
  • If it is going to an engineering audience, pick C, with the failing number kept.
  • If it is where the download link is shared, pick D.

Built as A into B into C into D: the gesture opens, the real app proves it, the numbers hold it up, and the four steps close it. That order is what site/demo.html implements.
